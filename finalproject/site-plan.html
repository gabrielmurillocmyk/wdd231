1. Site Name

Site Name: ApexAthletics Co.

Reason for Selection: The name reflects reaching your peak physical performance ("Apex") by utilizing reliable, high-durability classic athletic wear. It highlights physical utility, cross-sport functionality, and straightforward material durability without relying on confusing technical marketing buzzwords or electronics. It establishes a premium yet highly accessible identity.

Optional Domain Availability: apexathletics-co.org

2. Site Purpose

Site Purpose Statement: ApexAthletics Co. is an intuitive, responsive catalog platform showcasing standard high-quality training apparel built for durability. The primary purpose of the site content is to provide comprehensive information regarding essential athletic items—specifically reinforced workout shirts, supportive footwear, and high-strength shorts. The site guides users to durable options appropriate for general gym training, outdoor fitness, and the specific physical demands of four target sports: Soccer, Basketball, Baseball, and Volleyball.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

Our website layout and content elements are engineered to resolve specific, practical questions asked by real target audience users:

  • Scenario Question 1 (Footwear Focus): "I play intensive indoor volleyball and backyard basketball every single week. What pair of court shoes do you offer that feature reinforced ankle support and high-durability rubber outsoles to prevent tearing and sliding on rough surfaces?"
  • Scenario Question 2 (Apparel/Form Focus): "Where can I find a secure sizing selection page to submit an inquiry about bulk orders of tear-resistant, breathable training shirts for our local community youth baseball league?"

4. Color Scheme

At least two distinct theme colors are defined below to build solid visual repetition and design consistency:

  • Primary Color (#0b3c5d - Classic Deep Blue): Used for structural layout elements, section title lines, bold headings, container borders, and text emphasis points to anchor user attention.
  • Secondary Accent Color (#328cc1 - Active Blue): Applied to interactive navigation anchors, bullet point fragments, inline link hovers, and highlighting target sub-categories.
  • Athletic Gold Accent (#d9b310): Used carefully on call-to-action buttons, high-durability badges, or stock notices to prompt actions.
  • Background Space (#ffffff & #f9f9f9): Preserved for structural canvases to keep reading lines clear and manage spatial white-space parameters cleanly.
  • Core Body Text (#1d2731 - Charcoal Dark): A highly legible dark hue utilized exclusively on paragraph text blocks to verify top-tier accessibility contrast ratings.

5. Typography

Two safe, reliable fonts have been chosen to optimize reading speeds and eliminate unexpected structural shifts during page load:

  • Heading Font Selection: 'Trebuchet MS', sans-serif — Applied exclusively to all major header layers (<h1>, <h2>, and <h3> elements) to create clean visual presence and professional structure.
  • Body Font Selection: Arial, sans-serif — Applied to standard description paragraphs, list elements, form input text blocks, and table cells to yield optimal legibility across mobile and wide viewports.
